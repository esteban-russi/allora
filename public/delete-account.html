How to delete your account

  1. Open Allora (or visit [to confirm: production app URL]).
  2. Sign in to the account you want to delete.
  3. Tap your avatar to open the Account menu.
  4. Select Delete account and confirm Delete.
Active subscriptions are not cancelled automatically. Deleting your Allora account does not cancel a paid subscription. To stop future charges, cancel your web subscription through the management link in your receipt email (RevenueCat Web Billing / Stripe) or, for future Android subscriptions, in Google Play.

What is deleted

When you confirm deletion, Allora permanently deletes:

  • Your account record and Firebase Authentication login identity.
  • All learning sessions, chat conversations, voice-call transcripts, grammar notes, and saved notebook phrases.
  • All daily usage records (chat, voice, and helper request counters).
  • Your RevenueCat subscriber record (when billing is enabled).

What is kept

Payment, invoice, and tax records held by Stripe (and Google Play when Android subscriptions are available) are retained by those payment processors as required by applicable law.
