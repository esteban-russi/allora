Privacy Policy

How Allora collects, uses, and deletes your personal data.

1. Who we are and how to contact us

Allora is an AI-powered conversational Italian learning application operated by [to confirm: developer legal name and postal address].

If you have any questions about this policy or want to exercise your privacy rights, email us at estebangr2711@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect

  • Learner profile and preferences: your first name, chosen Italian CEFR level (A1–C1), selected tutor, and grammar feedback setting.
  • Account details: an account identifier, account creation and last-seen timestamps, and — when you sign in with Google or an email sign-in link — your email address. Guest sessions use an anonymous identifier without an email address.
  • Conversations and notebook: your text messages, tutor replies, translations, grammar notes, voice-call transcripts (up to the most recent 50 turns per session), CEFR level history, and phrases you save to your notebook.
  • Voice audio: when you use live voice calls or voice input, your audio is streamed or sent to our speech and AI providers to transcribe speech and generate spoken replies. Allora does not store your voice recordings.
  • Daily usage counts: per-UTC-day counts of chat messages, voice call seconds, and helper requests (translations, text-to-speech, and speech-to-text) used to apply daily plan limits.
  • Subscription status: your plan product ID, billing store (web, or Google Play when available), subscription status (active, grace period, or expired), trial or paid period type, expiry date, auto-renewal status, and billing management URL. We do not collect or store your payment card details.
  • Local browser storage: your current session ID (to resume your session), authentication state managed by Firebase, and your email address temporarily while completing an email-link sign-in.
  • Technical security data: IP addresses are processed in memory for rate limiting and abuse prevention. Allora uses self-hosted fonts and does not include third-party advertising or ad trackers.

3. Why we use your data

  • To provide conversational Italian chat, live voice calls, translations, grammar feedback, and your saved phrase notebook.
  • To remember your active session and progress across visits, and to transfer a guest session when you sign in.
  • To enforce fair daily usage limits for guest, free, and Allora Premium plans.
  • To verify and manage Allora Premium subscriptions.
  • To protect the service against abuse and keep it running reliably.

4. Service providers (processors)

We do not sell your personal data. We use the following service providers to run Allora:

  • Google Cloud: Vertex AI (Gemini models for chat replies, translations, live voice calls, text-to-speech, and fallback speech-to-text), Cloud Speech-to-Text, Cloud Text-to-Speech, Cloud Firestore (storing accounts, sessions, and daily usage counters), and Firebase Authentication (Google Sign-In, email-link sign-in, and anonymous guest accounts).
  • RevenueCat: subscription entitlement management and RevenueCat Web Billing checkout.
  • Stripe: payment processing for web subscriptions purchased through RevenueCat Web Billing.
  • Google Play: planned payment and subscription processing for Android when the Android app is available.

5. AI processing and model training

In production, Allora routes learner text and voice inputs to Google Cloud Vertex AI by default (vertex_only mode). Under Google Cloud Vertex AI enterprise terms, your prompts, conversations, and voice audio are not used to train Google’s models. Fallback to Google AI Studio is disabled by default for learner data.

6. How long we keep your data

  • Learning sessions (conversations, call transcripts, profile, and notebook): expire automatically after 30 days of inactivity (each activity resets the 30-day window; expired sessions are removed by Cloud Firestore TTL). Up to the last 50 turns are kept per session.
  • Anonymous guest account records: expire automatically after 30 days of inactivity (Cloud Firestore TTL).
  • Signed-in account records: kept until you delete your account.
  • Daily usage counters: expire automatically 35 days after the UTC day they were recorded (Cloud Firestore TTL).
  • Voice audio: processed in real time and not stored by Allora.
  • Payment records: transaction and tax records held by Stripe (and Google Play when Android billing is available) are retained by those providers as required by law.

7. Your rights and deleting your data

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, or to object to or restrict certain processing.

You can permanently delete your account and associated data directly in Allora at any time. See our Delete account page for step-by-step instructions, or email estebangr2711@gmail.com if you need assistance or cannot sign in.

8. Adults only

Allora is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has provided data to Allora, contact estebangr2711@gmail.com so we can delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Allora evolves. Any changes will be posted on this page, and material changes will be highlighted in the app.
